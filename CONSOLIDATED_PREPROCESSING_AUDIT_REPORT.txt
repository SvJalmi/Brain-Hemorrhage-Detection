6. LIMITATIONS AND REMAINING WORK

1. nnU-Net preprocessing is verified, but successful nnU-Net training and evaluation have not been completed.
2. Earlier small U-Net and imbalance-aware experiments collapsed to background and did not produce foreground predictions.
3. Full EfficientNet fine-tuning on the local CPU was stopped due to runtime constraints; frozen EfficientNet-B0 is the current classification baseline.
4. Segmentation must be evaluated on held-out test cases after successful training. Report class-wise Dice, sensitivity/recall, and suitable volume/error metrics, documenting empty-mask handling.
5. Reference annotation uncertainty must be acknowledged. annot_3 is a selected reference, not guaranteed ground truth.
6. Reference-mask volume analysis must not be described as automated model volume estimation.
7. Grad-CAM must not be presented as proof of accurate localization.
8. No clinical validation or clinical deployment claim is supported by these checks.

7. OVERALL AUDIT CONCLUSION

Based on the checks recorded here, preprocessing and data-integrity checks are complete for all three datasets, and nnU-Net 2D and 3D full-resolution preprocessing outputs and five-fold split configuration are present and consistent.

The remaining major technical task is successful segmentation model training and held-out evaluation. The current work supports reporting dataset preparation, leakage-safe splits, preprocessing validation, a baseline classification experiment, Grad-CAM visualization, and reference-mask lesion-volume analysis. It does not support claiming a successful segmentation model or clinical readiness.

End of report.
